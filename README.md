# MPCI Brazil: Growth Automation System

Interactive demo of the international prospection pipeline I designed and ran at MPCI Brazil: lead capture from LinkedIn Sales Navigator, email enrichment, Instantly outreach sequences, Calendly booking sync, a Notion CRM, and an auto-generated monthly report.

**Illustrative data:** company names and figures shown in the demo are fictional. They show how the system works and do not reflect MPCI prospects or results.

**Stack:** Instantly · Calendly · Notion CRM · Make / Zapier · LinkedIn Sales Navigator · Hunter / Apollo · Claude
